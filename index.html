Index vs. Raw Scan

Runs the same five searches two ways on a growing collection: opening and reading every document (raw scan) and reading only the postings lists of an inverted index. Works on any phone browser, with nothing to install.

1. Choose a collection size

The ten announcements (D1 to D10) are repeated to reach the chosen size, so the content stays the same and only the amount grows. Run each size three times for your Part 4B table.
